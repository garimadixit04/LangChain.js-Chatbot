This is sample data so you can try the app. Replace it with your own files.

## Trail X

The Trail X is an electric mountain bike with a 90 km range and a 5-year frame warranty. It is built by the E-Bikes team in the Lisbon workshop. The Trail X costs 3,200 euros.

## City Glide

The City Glide is a lightweight commuter bike for city streets. It is built by the Urban team in the Porto workshop. The City Glide costs 850 euros and comes in three colours.

## Refunds

Customers can return any bike within 30 days for a full refund. Refund requests are handled by the Customer Care team.
